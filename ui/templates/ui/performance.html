How these are calculated

Net capital committed
Deposits plus tax refunds, minus withdrawals to your own bank. Card spending is not subtracted: it is everyday spending, not capital taken back.
Lifetime P&L
Value today + card spending − net capital committed − dividends and interest received. What the money you committed gained from price changes alone.
Unrealised P&L
Value of current positions minus their average buy cost, as reported by Trade Republic.
XIRR
Money-weighted annual return over deposits, withdrawals and today's value. Because the account is also used for card payments, read it next to lifetime P&L.
Invested capital
Running total of buys minus sells. It is a cost basis, not a market value.
Forward income
The last 365 days of dividends, scaled to a full year when the history is shorter.

How this works

Actual
Cash plus positions on every day since the account opened. Rebuilt from your trades: shares from each trade's detail (or estimated from the amount and that day's price) times Trade Republic's closing prices. Days with a sync use Trade Republic's own figures.
Scenarios up to today
Your own deposits, withdrawals, card spending, buys and sells, replayed with the invested money earning the scenario's return. Cash keeps the interest you actually received. The gap to the actual line is how your investments did against that return.
After today
The monthly contribution is invested each month and raised by the yearly increase every twelve months. Invested money earns the scenario's return; the cash left earns the cash rate.
From today's value
Starts the three scenarios from your real cash and positions today instead of the replay.
Your investments returned
The single annual return on invested money that makes the replay land exactly on today's value.
Today's euros
Every amount deflated to today's prices with the euro-area HICP, then with the inflation assumption after its latest month.
